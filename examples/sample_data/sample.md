# AI Policy Overview

Our company is committed to responsible AI development. All AI systems must be audited quarterly and comply with data privacy regulations. Engineers are required to document model decisions and maintain explainability reports.

# Data Handling Guidelines

Customer data must never be used to train models without explicit written consent. All data processed by AI systems must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Retention periods must not exceed 90 days unless a legal hold is in place.

# Incident Response

Any AI system producing unexpected or harmful outputs must be immediately suspended pending investigation. The engineering team must file an incident report within 24 hours. A post-mortem must be completed within two weeks of resolution.
